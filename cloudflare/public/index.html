About these readings

Routine reports arrive every 6 hours; the station is overdue after 12 hours. Refreshing this page checks the cloud, not the sleeping detector.

The voltage and battery estimate use the last reported ADC reading. The percentage uses a generic 3.7 V LiPo curve, rounded to 5% steps. It is not a measured charge level or a runtime prediction. Temperature, load, charging and ADC error affect it; USB with no battery can still produce a reading. The device cannot confirm charging or battery presence. Compare voltage with a meter for calibration.

At an indicated 3.40 V or below, recharge: the detector skips Wi-Fi to conserve power. This threshold is separate from the percentage estimate. Queue overflow counts records lost before upload.
